## 🔍 Understanding Your Diagram

Here's a quick guide to what you'll see:

- **Circles/Nodes:** Represent files, folders, or modules
- **Lines/Arrows:** Show relationships and dependencies between them
- **Colors:** Different colors group related components together
- **Zoom Controls:** Use your mouse wheel or the + / - buttons to zoom in and out
- **Click a Node:** See a brief description of what that file does

The **explainer video** is like a short, narrated slideshow that walks you through the most important parts of the codebase — perfect for getting up to speed fast.

## 🧪 Troubleshooting

**Issue: The app won't open after double-clicking.**

- Make sure you've extracted the files completely (don't run it from inside the zip)
- Right-click the app and select **"Run as administrator"**
- Try restarting your computer

**Issue: The diagram is blank or never loads.**

- Check your internet connection
- Try a different repository (some might be too large)
- Refresh the app or restart it

**Issue: I get a warning about an unknown publisher.**

- This is normal for new software. Click **"More info"** → **"Run anyway"**
